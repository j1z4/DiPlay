DiPlay UI library
DiPlay · CarPlay, that you own.

A calm, minimal UI for the head unit.

This library maps out the DiPlay interface as tokens, components and screens. It takes the layout from the Figma frame, the focus model from Apple TV settings, and the information architecture from the current Android app. Every specimen on these pages is the real component, not a picture.

Principles

Glanceable

One idea per row, large type, high contrast on true black. A driver reads it in under a second.

Focus is the hero

Touch, rotary knob or D-pad: the focused item lifts and turns white. Nothing else competes with it.

Motion explains

Animations are 90–420 ms and show cause and effect: push, pop, swap, confirm. None loop for decoration.

Shape follows size

Corner radius comes from control height, the rule the app already uses. No button picks its own radius.

How to walk through it

Go page by page with the links at the bottom, or jump with the sidebar. The Walkthrough page is the whole app flow in a head-unit frame. Drive it with the mouse, or with ↑ ↓ ← →, Enter and Esc as a D-pad.

Foundations

Color

The canvas is true black, rows are graphite, and the home card is the Figma slate deepened into glass. Color carries meaning only: brand green means on or connected, amber means it needs attention, red means destructive.

Brand

Taken from the mark's gradient. Signal green is the only hue in the chrome. It means "on", "connected" and "you are here".

Foundations

Motion

Everything moves on five durations and three curves. Focus uses a soft spring that overshoots about 4%, which is what makes the buttons feel physical. Reduced-motion settings drop all of it to an instant cut.

MomentDurationCurve
Press90ms, scale 0.96spring on release
Focus move240ms, lift 1.035spring
Color change, value swap160–240msout
Screen push / pop420ms, 36px slide plus fade, rows staggered 40msout
Dialog420ms in, 160ms outspring in, in-out out
Edge bump220ms, 4pxspring. Shown when the D-pad hits the end of a list.

Logo and splash

The DiPlay mark is a rounded five-point asterisk drawn as one closed path, so it traces cleanly. The splash draws the outline, fills it with the signal-green gradient, then lets the outline go. The same timing works as an Android 12 splash screen and as a boot animation.

Components

Buttons

They are quiet by default. One primary (white) button per screen, the same inversion as a focused row, so the eye learns that white means "this one". A press squeezes the button to 96%. Focus springs it up and draws the ring.

Kinds

Sizes, icons and focus

Tab to a button, or hover it, to see the focus state. A focus ring only appears for keyboard or D-pad, never for touch, as in the app.

Components

Category rail

This is the left column of Settings. The current category gets a soft fill and the focused one inverts. On a head unit, moving focus down the rail swaps the content pane straight away, with no press needed, the way Apple TV does.

Components

Banner, toast, dialog

These are three levels of interruption. A toast confirms and leaves. A banner waits for an action but blocks nothing; it is the app's "Reconnect now" bar. A dialog stops everything and is used only before something that drops CarPlay.

Roadmap

Taking it to the app

The library is the spec. The tokens and rules port to Kotlin without new dependencies, because the app already builds views in code.

Suggested order

  1. Tokens. Add a NIGHT_MINIMAL DiPlayPalette with the values from Color, and put motion constants (durations, a PathInterpolator(0.22f, 1f, 0.36f, 1f) and an OvershootInterpolator(1.4f)) next to the spacing constants.
  2. Focus. Swap the 3dp ring on settings rows for the inverted fill and a ViewPropertyAnimator scale of 1.035 in an OnFocusChangeListener. Keep the ring on buttons.
  3. Rows and groups. Restyle button("Title · Value") rows and drop the card outline, with group headers above each card.
  4. Home. Rebuild home() as the Figma split with the device card.
  5. Pickers. Move choice dialogs to pushed pages with slide transitions.
  6. Waiting screen. Add the ripple indicator and the success pop.
  7. In-CarPlay menu. Reuse rows and the rail with overlay tokens.
Still open. Right-to-left (Arabic) mirroring of rows and the rail, the large font-scale layout, and the in-CarPlay overlay screens are not drawn yet. AGENTS.md asks for screenshots of each before a layout change ships.
